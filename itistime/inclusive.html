ORIGINAL CAMPAIGN PLATFORM · 07 / 09

An Inclusive Society

Preserved campaign proposals. These are historical statements, not current government commitments.

IN THIS ARTICLE

  1. Addressing Systemic Discrimination and protecting Vulnerable Groups
  2. Persons Living With Disability

Yemi Osinbajo recognises that an inclusive society is important for sustainable prosperity and will:
  • Ensure that women and young people fill a minimum of 35% of appointive positions;
  • Work with organised private sector to ensure that women represent 35% of leadership positions in the private sector;

Addressing Systemic Discrimination and protecting Vulnerable Groups


  • Review existing legislation and policy to eliminate systemic discrimination against vulnerable groups
  • Work with States to ensure the full domestication of the Child Rights Act
  • Ensure the full implementation of the Violence Against Persons Prohibition Act to secure greater protection against domestic violence, gender and sexual based violence;

Persons Living With Disability


  • The full Implementation of the Disability Act to ensure that Persons Living With Disabilities have equal opportunity to live full and rounded lives.
