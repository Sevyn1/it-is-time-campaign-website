Leadership in Africa


Yemi Osinbajo will:
  • Maintain Nigeria's leadership position within Africa and work to deepen Nigeria's role in regional integration.
  • Promote good governance and Facilitate Peace and Security through active engagement and the use of his good offices among neighbours in the region.

The Africa Continental Free Trade Agreement (AFCFTA) for Jobs & SMEs


  • Through specific policy and value chain support, Yemi Osinbajo will improve our comparative advantage to position Nigerian businesses to capture a significant portion of the African market.
