About Prof

Meet Professor Yemi Osibanjo, Our Next President

Born into the family of Opeoluwa Osinbajo on the 8th On the 8th of March 1957, Yemi Osinbajo was born to Opeoluwa and Olubisi Osinbajo at Creek hospital in Lagos, Nigeria. He had his primary school education at Corona Primary School, Lagos, and then proceeded to Igbobi College, Lagos, for his secondary school education from 1969 to 1975. As a student of Igbobi College, he won the following awards: The State Merit Award (1971); the School Prize for English Oratory (1972); Adeoba Prize for English Oratory (1972-1975); Elias Prize for Best Performance in History (WASC, 1973); School Prize for Literature (HSC, 1975); and African Statesman Intercollegiate Best Speaker’s Prize (1974).

THE PLAN

A Unified Country

A country divided will not stand and we understand that. That is why Yemi Osinbajo will unify the nation as one cohesive yet diverse unit. People will not be discriminated against on the basis of ethnicity, religion, gender.

A Safe Country For All

Yemi Osibanjo has made our security a priority and will continue to invest in building the capacity of our security forces and win the battle against banditry, terrorism, extremism, kidnapping and other anarchical enterprises.

Infrastructure

Yemi Osinbajo will continue to enable investments in our infrastructure until the whole of Nigeria is completely integrated, such that people, goods and services can flow freely within, as well as to and from Nigeria.

Investing in People

Yemi Osinbajo will Intentionally invest in the development of practical, creative and innovative skills for young Nigerians that will become the catalyst for Nigeria’s emerging knowledge economy.

An Inclusive Society

Yemi Osinbajo recognises that an inclusive society is important for sustainable prosperity and will ensure the review of existing legislations and policies to eliminate systemic discrimination against vulnerable groups.

Achieving Energy Security for Nigeria

Yemi Osinbajo will improve our energy efficiency by optimising our current installed capacity, harnessing natural gas reserves and diversifying our energy mix.

Jobs and Opportunity

With a large youth population, Nigeria is in a strong position to build a workforce that is skilled, competent and successful. To do this, Yemi Osinbajo will prioritise the Manufacturing, Technology, Agriculture and Construction sectors.

Foreign Policy

We aim to maintain Nigeria’s leadership position in Africa and work to deepen Nigeria’s role in regional integration. We will improve our comparative advantage to help Nigerian businesses to capture a significant portion of Africa's market.

Investment Climate

Yemi Osinbajo is committed to ensuring that everyone business in Nigeria, from multinationals, large corporations and established businesses to tech start-ups, and small businesses, is able to go about their operations seamlessly and with ease.
